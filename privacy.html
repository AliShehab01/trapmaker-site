Trapmaker

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026. Trapmaker is developed by Ali Shehab in Bahrain. Contact: alishehab.tech@gmail.com.

Trapmaker is preparing its iPhone release. Some prerelease builds have online services, purchases or advertising disabled. This policy describes the features when enabled; disabled features do not send their associated data.

Local play

You can play the campaign and create local challenges without an account. Progress, settings and saved drafts stay on your device. Deleting the app removes its local saves; reinstalling may not recover them. Apple purchases can be restored separately.

Apple sign-in and community challenges

When community services are connected, Sign in with Apple authenticates publishing, likes, following creators, reports and verified records. We use an account identifier and a generated creator name. We do not request your name or email from Apple. A sign-in credential may contain an email claim; it is processed by the authentication provider.

Public challenges contain your generated creator name, level title and layout, publication date, likes and gameplay statistics. Run commands and results are sent to the verification service to check completion and calculate records and difficulty. These commands describe game controls, not recordings from your camera or microphone. Your follows, blocks and reports are used to provide those features.

Online authentication and community storage use Supabase when configured, together with our verification-service hosting provider. Servers process connection information such as IP addresses and request times for delivery, security and abuse prevention. We do not include secret service credentials in the game.

Shared links

A local challenge code contains the level and a self-reported time. Anyone receiving it can read and reshare it. Community links identify a published level and its public statistics. Copies saved by other players cannot be recalled by removing the original.

Advertising

Google AdMob serves optional rewarded ads for extra gold or a retry, and occasional interstitial ads at breaks between completed rounds. The game does not display interstitials during active gameplay. An ad may be unavailable; no reward is granted for an incomplete rewarded ad.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location, device identifiers including the advertising identifier when permitted, ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information. These support advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Read Google's partner information, Google's privacy policy and Google's SDK data disclosure.

Where required, Google's consent form appears before requesting ads. Settings contains Privacy options when applicable. iOS may separately ask for tracking permission. Declining tracking does not block gameplay or purchases. Buying Remove Ads disables advertising and provides the same limited bonus/retry rewards directly. Its price is shown by the App Store in your local currency.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and payments. We do not receive your card details. The game processes purchase identifiers and entitlement information to provide and restore Remove Ads. A cached entitlement is stored locally. Refunds and revoked purchases may remove that entitlement.

Retention and deletion

Local drafts remain until you delete them or the app. Community account data and published levels remain until removed through the community service or account deletion. Security logs and backups may remain for the hosting provider's operational retention period; removed content may also be retained where necessary to investigate abuse or meet legal obligations.

When online accounts are enabled, use the community account menu to request account deletion inside the app. It asks for fresh Apple authorization to revoke the linked sign-in before deleting the account and associated community data. You can also request help or exercise applicable access, correction or deletion rights by email. Signing out alone does not delete published content.

Children and public content

Trapmaker is not designed for children under 13. Do not publish personal information in a level title. Report inappropriate content or block its creator from the challenge details page. There is no player-to-player chat.

International processing and changes

Service providers may process data outside your country under their applicable safeguards. We update this page when the game's data practices change. Contact us with privacy questions before enabling online features if you need clarification.
