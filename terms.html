Community rules

Last updated: 16 October 2026. These rules apply when Trapmaker's community features are enabled. For help: alishehab.tech@gmail.com.

  • Publish only levels you created and completed under the current game rules. Replays are verified by the service; manipulated results may be rejected.
  • Do not include personal information, harassment, hateful or sexual content, threats, unlawful material, advertisements, impersonation or content that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Use the Report and Block Creator controls in challenge details. Reports are reviewed by the operator; content may be hidden while reviewed. Abuse can result in removal of content or restriction of an account.
  • Publishing makes your level, generated creator name and gameplay statistics public. You allow Trapmaker to host and display that content to players. Do not submit material you cannot share.
  • Difficulty is estimated from verified attempts after enough plays. A difficulty label is separate from likes or quality.
  • Availability, records and features can change as the game evolves. Back up local level codes you want to keep.

Purchases and ads

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase. See the App Store purchase sheet for your price. Optional rewards remain limited by game rules; purchasing does not create unlimited currency. Payments and refund requests are handled through Apple. The game remains playable without buying Remove Ads or watching rewarded ads.

Account deletion

When signed in, open the community account controls and choose Delete Account. Fresh Apple authorization is required to revoke the linked sign-in. This removes associated community data; local drafts and Apple purchase history are separate.

The app's standard licence is Apple's Standard EULA.
