Trapmaker

Support

Write to alishehab.tech@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Never send passwords, verification codes, sign-in tokens or payment-card details.

Restore Remove Ads

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases using the Apple account that bought it. Apple controls purchase availability and refunds. The intended US launch price is $9.99; the store's purchase sheet is authoritative.

Build and share

Hold the phone sideways. Drag the battlefield to pan, pinch or use + / − to zoom, and use Editor for undo, redo or a new map. Complete your exact level before publishing. Your friends can open a community link or import a local challenge code.

Online community

Offline building and campaign play do not need an account. Apple sign-in is required for publishing, ratings and verified records when services are connected. Prerelease builds may show “Community is not connected yet.”

Report content or delete your account

Use Report or Block Creator in challenge details. For urgent concerns email the support address with the challenge ID. The community account menu provides Sign Out and Delete Account; deletion asks for fresh Apple authorization.

No ad is available

Rewarded ads depend on connection, region, consent and availability. Try again later; watching an ad is optional. Privacy options appears in Settings when required by the consent provider.
